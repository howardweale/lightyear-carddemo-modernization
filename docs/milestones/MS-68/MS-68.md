# MS #68 - iDempiere Dialect Inventory and Pairing

> **Status:** Complete — deterministic source-bound evidence
>
> **Release:** 0.68.0
>
> **Date:** 2026-09-11
>
> **Audience:** Customers, partners, executives, architects, delivery teams, and assurance reviewers

## Executive summary

MS #68 - iDempiere Dialect Inventory and Pairing converts a specific part of the LIGHTYEAR modernization approach into a governed, reviewable capability. Accounts for every current Oracle and PostgreSQL migration script in the pinned iDempiere estate and gives the next comparison stage a reproducible coverage denominator.

## Why this matters to a customer

Accounts for every current Oracle and PostgreSQL migration script in the pinned iDempiere estate and gives the next comparison stage a reproducible coverage denominator.

The practical result is a narrower, evidence-backed decision instead of a broad modernization claim. The milestone packages capability, proof, and limitations together so commercial, architecture, delivery, security, and assurance teams can review the same record.

## What the milestone delivers

- All 1,078 Oracle and 1,078 PostgreSQL current migration scripts paired at the existing release-13 commit, with no unpaired files.
- A deterministic pairing manifest with 1,077 exact release and filename matches and one unique same-release IDEMPIERE-5963 ticket match.
- A 93-pair order-to-cash pilot selected from lexical references to the existing nine-table business slice.
- Logical and transport hashes for every script, plus 31 observed logical-content-identical pairs without a semantic-equivalence verdict.
- A nine-file database-provider and conversion boundary, including the logger that writes both Oracle and PostgreSQL migration outputs.
- A content-addressed Stage 1 receipt, two schemas, source-bound rebuild verification, POSIX and PowerShell launchers, and tests for tampering and unsupported claims.

## How it differs from earlier work

MS #48 pinned and inventoried iDempiere, MS #52 projected the estate, and MS #33 established the database semantic core. MS #68 reuses those assets to audit paired dialect outputs. MS #69 will compare semantics and MS #70 will triage findings and assemble evidence. CloudBank production-readiness work remains unnumbered and is not repeated here.

Earlier work remains useful evidence, but it is not automatically promoted into this milestone. A successful parser, simulator, local comparison, or generated artifact is not presented as live platform equivalence or production readiness.

## What a customer can do with it

A customer can use idempiere dialect inventory and pairing as a bounded decision and delivery capability. It can be attached to the customer's approved source package, owners, policies, target architecture, acceptance criteria, and authorized evidence.

Unresolved semantic loss, policy decisions, unsupported behavior, and live-evidence requirements remain visible rather than being averaged into one pass/fail statement.

## Evidence and acceptance posture

The milestone is complete as a repository capability because its committed implementation and release record are present. Customer qualification remains claim-specific: only the delivered behaviors listed above are supported, and only at the evidence class recorded by their underlying receipts and gates.

A customer review should confirm:

1. the exact source, graph, policy, fixture, and artifact identities referenced by the evidence;
2. the difference between deterministic development evidence and authorized live evidence;
3. every policy decision, lossy transformation, unsupported behavior, and explicit exclusion;
4. the acceptance gates that passed and the live or production gates that remain blocked; and
5. that later changes have not invalidated the content-addressed evidence.

## Boundaries and claims not made

- Stage 1 proves file-level pairing coverage and pilot selection for one exact public-source commit; it does not prove parser coverage or semantic equivalence.
- Independent hand maintenance is unproven because iDempiere includes a converter and a dual-dialect migration logger. Per-pair provenance remains unclassified.
- The pilot selector is lexical and does not yet distinguish executable references from comments.
- No model was called and Builder remained unused. Deterministic comparison and bounded triage remain planned work.
- No native database execution, whole-application equivalence, migration completion, customer readiness or production readiness is claimed.

These boundaries are part of the deliverable. They prevent bounded development evidence from becoming an unsupported customer promise.
