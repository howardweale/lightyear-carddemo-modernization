# MS92 Frozen factory repeatability experiment

[Word edition](MS-92.docx)

**Status:** Completed experiment; zero verified successes in 40 planned trials. **Date:** 28 September 2026.

MS92 tested whether the previous single successful journey would repeat under frozen conditions. All 40 slots ran and halted. This establishes that the tested factory configuration was not reliable enough to claim repeatable autonomous delivery. It does not establish that reliable delivery is impossible: the experiment also exposed defects in the judge, public contract and execution equipment that MS93 addresses separately.

## Experiment and observed outcomes

Ten fresh generations ran in each of four conditions. Baseline repeated the operations journey; procure-to-pay introduced purchasing; the other two conditions removed the decimal-format instruction or Boolean hint separately. Conditions were interleaved. Each trial had at most five client calls; the cohort allowed 200 calls and 40 local execution hours. Its declaration, controller, judge and inputs were fixed before execution. No trial was replaced or restarted.

| Condition | Observed result |
|---|---|
| Baseline | 0 first-try passes; 0 repaired passes; 10 halts |
| Procure to pay | 0 first-try passes; 0 repaired passes; 10 halts |
| Without decimal instruction | 0 first-try passes; 0 repaired passes; 10 halts |
| Without Boolean hint | 0 first-try passes; 0 repaired passes; 10 halts |

Every condition had zero invalid slots and zero unstarted slots under the original controller's classification. Across the cohort, 38 trials halted without supported repair feedback and two exhausted their frozen call limit. Twenty-six trials used repairs, producing 38 revised candidates. None passed the combined gate.

The observed pass rate was 0 of 10 in each condition. The report's descriptive 95 percent Wilson interval is 0 to about 27.8 percent per condition under a binomial working model. Small samples, shared inputs and provider drift limit generalization. Conditions must not be pooled to claim a general factory success rate or to estimate the causal value of either removed hint.

The requested model was gpt-6-astra with high reasoning, through the pinned Codex CLI. Local electricity and CPU cost were not metered.

### Why the halts matter

Fifty-five native attempts ended in harness exceptions and 23 in gate failures. None produced a complete combined gate file under the old implementation. Signed publications establish retained artifact identity and provenance; a verified archive is not a passed native comparison. The original controller's zero-invalid count does not mean every judge execution completed normally.

Investigation found ambiguous invoice-line assumptions, purchasing costing side effects, missing or duplicate trace entries and compiler feedback the exporter did not recognize. A halted candidate therefore cannot automatically be described as a proven business defect. MS92 preserves the original classifications; MS93 introduces explicit execution-failure, judge-error and insufficient-evidence results for subsequent work.

### Builder input provenance

Baseline inherited MS86's API and locking examples, MS88/MS89's Boolean, posting-signature and no-reposting lessons, and MS89/MS90's decimal-format lesson. Purchasing used the predeclared purchasing contract. The ablations removed one named instruction each; no campaign-specific lesson was added after generation began. Zero repair bytes excludes development of those inherited inputs and of the judge and controller.

The 40 complete archives total 2,499,047,692 bytes and remain at the recorded local publication paths. They are not embedded in this PR. A fresh clone can inspect and authenticate the compact results; full artifact reconstruction requires those retained archives and the trusted operator key. Signatures are attribution and tamper evidence, not independent attestation.

### Commercial significance

The experiment replaces a single success story with a reproducibility measurement and preserves failures that can guide development. It does not support an autonomous production-readiness claim. The next engineering priority is to qualify the judge and execution environment with a known reference and deliberate faults before measuring the factory again.
