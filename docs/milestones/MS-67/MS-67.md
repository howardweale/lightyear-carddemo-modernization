## Executive summary

MS #67 - CloudBank Real Non-Production Platform Qualification converts a specific part of the LIGHTYEAR modernization approach into a governed, reviewable capability. Turns the deployment design into a deployable managed-cloud platform and makes operational claims depend on signed live Kubernetes evidence rather than architecture documents.

## What the milestone delivers

- A signed MS #65/MS #66 receipt chain and signed platform profile bound to one real, explicitly authorized non-production context.
- An ephemeral regional GKE Standard implementation with private nodes across three zones, Workload Identity, Dataplane V2, bounded administrator access, Artifact Registry, Cloud DNS, and explicit teardown.
- Cloud SQL PostgreSQL regional high availability, automated backup and point-in-time recovery, exact restored-state comparison, and bounded RPO/RTO evidence.
- Digest-only Java 21 service images with verified OpenTelemetry agent input, Cloud KMS Cosign signatures and provenance, and Trivy scanning that admits no critical or high findings.
- Secret Manager through External Secrets Operator without static Google service-account keys or committed values, including synchronization and rotation evidence for all eight services.
- Trusted public TLS through cert-manager and ingress-nginx; OpenTelemetry metrics and traces, Cloud Logging, all-service correlation, and alert fire/recovery evidence.
- A minimum five-minute, 1,000-request, concurrency-10 k6 window with zero errors and p95 no greater than 500 milliseconds.
- Controlled node and failure-domain evacuation, zero-unavailable rolling deployment for all eight services, canary cutover, all 18 business journeys, mandatory rollback, and recovery.
- Twenty-eight exact signed scenarios, four schemas, minimized evidence receipts, cross-platform admission launchers, GKE scripts and runbook, CI, Control Tower projection, and adversarial tests.

## How it differs from earlier work

MS #65 designed and admitted a production-like rehearsal, while MS #66 proved bounded eight-service business equivalence. MS #67 chains both results into the first real managed Kubernetes platform implementation and preserves customer-specific production certification for MS #68.

Earlier work remains useful evidence, but it is not automatically promoted into this milestone. A successful parser, simulator, local comparison, or generated artifact is not presented as live platform equivalence or production readiness.

## What a customer can do with it

A customer can use cloudbank real non-production platform qualification as a bounded decision and delivery capability. It can be attached to the customer's approved source package, owners, policies, target architecture, acceptance criteria, and authorized evidence.

Unresolved semantic loss, policy decisions, unsupported behavior, and live-evidence requirements remain visible rather than being averaged into one pass/fail statement.

## Evidence and acceptance posture

The milestone is complete as a repository capability because its committed implementation and release record are present. Customer qualification remains claim-specific: only the delivered behaviors listed above are supported, and only at the evidence class recorded by their underlying receipts and gates.

A customer review should confirm:

1. the exact source, graph, policy, fixture, and artifact identities referenced by the evidence;
2. the difference between deterministic development evidence and authorized live evidence;
3. every policy decision, lossy transformation, unsupported behavior, and explicit exclusion;
4. the acceptance gates that passed and the live or production gates that remain blocked; and
5. that later changes have not invalidated the content-addressed evidence.

## Boundaries and claims not made

- These boundaries describe MS #67's own evidence scope. Later MS65, MS66 and MS67 receipts add their separate nonproduction qualifications; they do not rewrite this milestone's signed fields.
- The controlled failure-domain evacuation is not represented as an actual Google Cloud zone outage.
- Customer IdP, representative customer data volume and workload, the customer's formal approval process, migration completion, production deployment, and final signed production readiness remain MS #68.

These boundaries are part of the deliverable. They prevent bounded development evidence from becoming an unsupported customer promise.
