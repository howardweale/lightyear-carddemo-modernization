## What a result can establish

A useful result identifies an exact source, candidate, observer, input set,
comparison policy and actual outcome. Its scope ends at those identities.
A twin comparison is different from a native-platform comparison. An authored
public fixture is different from a private customer workload. A component check
is different from end-to-end qualification.

The [P3 evidence rules](p3-operational-claims.md#sha256=57ad661029a58c3205bd3dd29f748f357b2f6835014464e0be0d9ee1885d2aa3)
govern eventual operational assertions. Planned: link each clause to a committed
public JSON receipt with its exact byte digest and matching category. A summary,
badge or metadata wrapper alone cannot establish factual truth or currentness.
