Daily exact-hash match strip and a source-linked divergence register. Keep twin-scoped matches separate from z/OS-facing confirmation. Proposed promotion and rollback decisions must bind a deployment, observation interval, comparison policy and complete divergence inventory. operator review. Customer/Maintec evidence remains outside this scope. Acceptance
requires tamper/stale-head/role tests and explicit separation of measured results
from approvals. This design grants no release, measurement or execution credit.
