# MS #70 - iDempiere Bounded Triage and Evidence Assembly

> **Status:** Complete bounded safe-floor package; live model run remains gated
>
> **Release:** 0.70.0
>
> **Date:** 2026-09-11
>
> **Audience:** Customers, partners, executives, architects, delivery teams, and assurance reviewers

## Executive summary

MS #70 packages twenty representative MS #69 findings behind hard model budgets and an authoritative deterministic verifier. The committed evidence records no live model run, preserves all 1,077 findings as indeterminate, and assembles complete coverage and claim-boundary registers.

## Why this matters to a customer

Turns the large unresolved comparison surface into a reviewable, budgeted work package and complete finding register without allowing model output to overrule deterministic evidence.

The practical result is a narrower, evidence-backed decision instead of a broad modernization claim. The milestone packages capability, proof, and limitations together so commercial, architecture, delivery, security, and assurance teams can review the same record.

## What the milestone delivers

- A deterministic sample of 20 unique flagged pairs across ten repeated reason strata, balanced between the frozen order-to-cash pilot and the remaining estate.
- Source-hash-bound context hydration limited to implicated ranges plus two lines, 48 lines per dialect and 80,000 bytes per role.
- Audit-specific strict Planner and Analyst schemas with 20-call role ceilings, 60,000 input-token and 25,000 output-token per-call limits, and a USD 200 aggregate cost ceiling.
- A deterministic verifier that rejects unbound evidence and reasons, preserves every MS #69 semantic verdict, and downgrades unsupported divergence proposals to indeterminate.
- A safe-floor role-contract calibration, full 1,077-pair indeterminate register, zero-entry divergence register, sampled provenance register and exact coverage denominator.
- A content-addressed receipt and schema, cross-platform build and verification commands, optional credentialed live-run plumbing, source replay and adversarial tests.

## How it differs from earlier work

MS #68 supplied the exact pairing and source pin, and MS #69 supplied source-range semantic findings. MS #70 reuses the existing bounded-provider controls, adds audit-specific Planner and Analyst contracts, and keeps Builder absent and deterministic verification authoritative.

Earlier work remains useful evidence, but it is not automatically promoted into this milestone. A successful parser, simulator, local comparison, or generated artifact is not presented as live platform equivalence or production readiness.

## What a customer can do with it

A customer can use idempiere bounded triage and evidence assembly as a bounded decision and delivery capability. It can be attached to the customer's approved source package, owners, policies, target architecture, acceptance criteria, and authorized evidence.

Unresolved semantic loss, policy decisions, unsupported behavior, and live-evidence requirements remain visible rather than being averaged into one pass/fail statement.

## Evidence and acceptance posture

The milestone is complete as a repository capability because its committed implementation and release record are present. Customer qualification remains claim-specific: only the delivered behaviors listed above are supported, and only at the evidence class recorded by their underlying receipts and gates.

A customer review should confirm:

1. the exact source, graph, policy, fixture, and artifact identities referenced by the evidence;
2. the difference between deterministic development evidence and authorized live evidence;
3. every policy decision, lossy transformation, unsupported behavior, and explicit exclusion;
4. the acceptance gates that passed and the live or production gates that remain blocked; and
5. that later changes have not invalidated the content-addressed evidence.

## Boundaries and claims not made

- The committed calibration records zero model calls and is not evidence of live Planner or Analyst performance.
- The twenty-case sample is not the entire 1,077-pair flagged set; all flagged semantic verdicts remain indeterminate.
- The zero-entry divergence register means no divergence is proven by the bounded deterministic evidence, not that none exists.
- Per-pair maintenance provenance remains unclassified because no source-history evidence is admitted.
- Builder is not used, no upstream source is edited, and no database is executed.
- Audit completion, native execution, application equivalence, migration completion, customer readiness and production readiness remain false. Community contact was not authorized or performed.

These boundaries are part of the deliverable. They prevent bounded development evidence from becoming an unsupported customer promise.
