Recorded October 15 2026. Implementation: PRs #342 through #349. This record extends MS104 and MS106.

LIGHTYEAR now checks the conditions behind a release decision before that decision can be signed or consumed. Customers and reviewers can trace the conclusion to specific source files, measurements and approvals.

## Proposed policy and guarded execution

Threshold/2 requires two observed values per output field. Seven source-assigned constants cannot meet that requirement. Howard selected preparation of threshold/3 with explicit constant-field exemptions. The unsigned proposal binds each exemption to the source, line and mutation evidence. It exempts only the diversity requirement; the fields must still be compared and mutation-tested. Threshold/3 is not implemented or activated by these merges, and INTCALC remains unreleasable under unchanged threshold/2.

POSTTRAN Phase 2 now requires a signed proof bound to its plan, candidate, compiler image and trusted journal before reserving its one permitted attempt. The downloaded image must match its hash and have at least 24 hours of retention. Equal return codes 0 or 4 may agree; equal 8 or 12 diverge. Candidate 999 versus twin 12 is allowed only for the declared duplicate-ID scenario. No Phase 2 comparison was authorized or run by this delivery. Integration CI found that the retained plan predates the new scope implementation. Preparation now validates the retained files at their recorded commit and reports current drift. Execution from the integrated checkout still refuses that drift and requires a new reviewed plan.
