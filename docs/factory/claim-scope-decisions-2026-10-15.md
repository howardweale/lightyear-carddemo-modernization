# Explicit claim scopes

The dated JSON overlays the retained argument and acceptance records without changing them. Each entry binds the mutant, original record hash and source hash. Unmatched or missing scope cannot exclude a survivor from a z/OS-facing denominator.

Existing argument scopes are transcribed into explicit data: host/compiler-dependent arguments are `host-compiler-scoped`; the remaining stated arguments are `twin-and-zos`. This grants no new equivalence acceptance. The two explicitly documented October 11 cross-platform amendments are retained as separate acceptance records; October 12 individual supplements remain twin-only. A future change of scope needs its own reviewed decision.

The engine and independent cross-check join these records separately. English wording no longer selects a scope. Existing host-semantics language remains a consistency check that fails when it contradicts or lacks a host-scoped argument record. “Not z/OS-specific” is not treated as a host-only declaration.
