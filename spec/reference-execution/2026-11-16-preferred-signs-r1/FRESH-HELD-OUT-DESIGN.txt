Reference preferred-sign limit (no PR yet, r1): proposed held-out constraint

Supersedes only the input-domain portion of the 15 November fresh held-out
design. REFERENCE_LIMIT_PREFERRED_SIGNS is pending Howard's decision.
The generator must encode every signed zoned DISPLAY field with preferred C/D
signs, including signed zero, using the reviewed copybook layout. Unsigned
numeric fields retain their normal F zones. Non-preferred signed zones are
refused before reference execution, never silently normalised or resampled.
Zone-F signed input families need mainframe evidence and are outside this
reference-backed set's proven domain. This is not full NUMPROC(NOPFD) coverage.
The generator and encoder need independent synthetic vectors and a whole-record
domain check, including later records and negative/positive zero. Invalid
generated bytes are a generator fault; no seed replacement or selection by
candidate performance. Signed separate/unsupported layouts remain refused.

Howard alone holds the private seed; no agent handles it. Expected answers come
from qualified GnuCOBOL execution of unmodified public COBOL, never from the Python
oracle. Inputs and answers are encrypted at first persistent write; no readable
copy enters a public CI artifact or Release. Only reviewed commitments and
ciphertext metadata are public. Claude reviews generator code, not private
output. Public families trace to public source, not retired-case specifics.
All other isolation, independent qualification, encryption, no fallback and
separate Howard gate-migration/freeze approvals remain required. This proposal
implements no generator, accepts no seed and enables no freeze.
Named limit: open-source reference, not mainframe-proven.
