# Lightyear product marks

Three product marks that sit under the LIGHTYEAR brand. Same badge for each: deep navy (#15184D) rounded square, 90-unit viewBox, corner radius 18.

| Product | Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Lightyear Prism | Light enters a glass prism and splits into a spectrum | Legacy goes in dark and comes out as visible, validated behaviour |
| Lightyear Aperture | An iris opening onto warm light | The opening anyone outside Lightyear uses to check the evidence |
| Lightyear Factory | A factory with glowing code inside, output on a conveyor | AI agents write the code, under signed limits |

Files
- `*-icon.svg`: source marks (use these wherever SVG is accepted).
- `*-icon.png`: 512 x 512 exports, transparent corners.
- `*-lockup.png`: icon + bronze LIGHTYEAR eyebrow + violet product name + tagline, 1344 px wide (2x), for documents.

Usage
- The LIGHTYEAR primary logo stays the company mark; product marks never replace it.
- Keep product marks small and off-centre in documents; do not recolour, stretch or add effects.
- The spectral colours inside Prism and the conveyor blocks in Factory are illustration colours, not brand tokens.
