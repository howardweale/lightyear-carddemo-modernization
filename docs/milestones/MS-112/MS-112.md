## What was delivered

POSTTRAN Phase 2, the signed comparison of the POSTTRAN candidate against the original, now has an enforced window.

- **The window:** the plan must be signed and launched by 29 October 2026 at 14:46 UTC.
- **Daily check:** a CI check runs every day against the real clock and opens a single warning issue as the deadline approaches.
- **After expiry:** the committed plan and amendment can still be verified once the CI artifact has expired, using the retained bytes.
- **Closing the warning:** the warning closes when the plan is consumed, when readiness is formally withdrawn, or when the window expires.
- **Runbook:** the PowerShell steps for import and proof are self-contained. They check the Tower's public-key fingerprint before import.

## Provenance finding

PR #397 audited the existing POSTTRAN records against the factory-build criteria adopted on 18 October.

- **Finding:** attempt 1 of the POSTTRAN candidate was generated by a single operator-started model call. It was not a full dark-factory run.
- **What the records don't show:**

  - a factory commit identity;
