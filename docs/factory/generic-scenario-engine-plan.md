# Generic scenario engine — plan for Howard, 2026-10-11

Status: proposed design only. Howard's review is required before implementation. The existing generator is renamed `curated-finite-witness`; its hand-selected cases and finite-domain checks are not a generic engine.

The engine will accept only a source-hashed COBOL program, its bound copybooks and dataset/key bindings, and the decision inventory. No program-name branches or hand-written scenarios are allowed in the acceptance run. A typed intermediate representation will retain statement locations, assignments, conditions, PIC/USAGE, data-flow edges and unsupported constructs. Layout-derived domains include representable limits, zero, signed units and arithmetic rounding straddles; record construction uses the same bound layouts and validates every encoded field.

Condition translation will propagate assignments to input fields and encode supported arithmetic, comparisons, Boolean expressions and bounded record relationships. Z3 is the preferred solver, pinned by version and hash; bounded exhaustive search is an explicit fallback. Unsupported conditions and uncertain aliasing, external calls, string/collation semantics and stateful I/O receive individual reasons. Finite-domain exhaustion is not an unreachability proof. Solver evidence and exact source bindings must survive in receipts.

A coverage loop will compile instrumented public host programs, target uncovered outcomes, retain killing/covering witnesses and stop on budget exhaustion. Proposed per-program engineering budget: 1,000 generated cases, 200 solver targets, 30 seconds per solver target, four hours total, 8 GiB output, zero models and no native B06 work. These are proposed limits, not authority to run a new evidence window. Fault-injection operational outcomes remain separate from business outcomes; accepted exceptions and keep-or-fix dependencies are explicit and invalidate on decision/source changes.

Acceptance uses CBTRN03C and CBSTM03A, held out from development: zero hand-written scenarios, complete input-layout and condition translation census, separate business/I/O coverage, a fresh systematic mutation campaign with all refusals/survivors retained, and an individual gaps register. Success is demonstrated generic generation and honest coverage/mutation receipts, not an assumed 100% score. Undocumented adapters or a program-specific case added to rescue a result fail this acceptance condition.

Implementation sequence after review: (1) typed parser and layout/data-flow translation tests; (2) solver and record construction; (3) bounded feedback and provenance receipts; (4) held-out compiled campaigns and report. Review needed: approve this scope/budget and the solver dependency. The independent B1 equivalence evidence and B2 register/scenario corrections proceed now under the existing authorization.
